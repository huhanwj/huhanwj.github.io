IERG2060 Draw · Privacy

This page is a teaching tool for IERG2060 tutorial groups. It is run by the course's teaching assistant (huhanwj@gmail.com).

Teaching assistants

"Sign in with Google" shares only your Google account's email address. It is used to decide which tutorial group you may open and is kept for at most two hours in the sign-in session. It is not stored in any spreadsheet.

Students

When you volunteer, the SID you enter is checked against the class list. Only your roster number is recorded for the draw; the SID itself is not stored by the sign-up service. Draw results (roster number, question number, date) are kept in the teaching team's private Google spreadsheet for course records.

Sharing

Nothing is sold or shared outside the course teaching team. Questions: huhanwj@gmail.com.
